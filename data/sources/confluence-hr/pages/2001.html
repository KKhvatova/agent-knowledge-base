Vacation and Time Off – Canada

Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 15 days of paid vacation per calendar year, accrued monthly.

Carry-over

Up to 5 unused days can be carried over and must be used by March 31.

How to request vacation

  1. Submit the request in Workday at least 2 weeks in advance.
  2. Your manager approves it within 3 business days.
